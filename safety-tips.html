SAFETY AWARENESS

Safety Tips

Simple safety practices that can help you stay prepared and connected.

01

Keep Your Phone Ready

Keep your phone charged and accessible, especially when travelling or going somewhere unfamiliar.

02

Stay Connected

Let someone you trust know where you are going when appropriate, especially during late or unfamiliar journeys.

03

Share Your Location

When you feel unsafe, consider sharing your location with a trusted person using a reliable location-sharing service.

04

Choose Safer Routes

Prefer well-lit and populated routes and stay aware of your surroundings.

05

Know Emergency Numbers

Keep important emergency services available so that you can contact appropriate help quickly.

Emergency 112 Ambulance 108
06

Trust Your Instincts

If a situation feels unsafe, move toward a safer place and contact someone you trust when possible.

Need Immediate Help?

If you are in immediate danger, contact appropriate emergency services.

Call 112
